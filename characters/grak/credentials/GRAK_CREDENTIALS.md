### Seven roads

1. **GR1 - Grammar.** Morphemes, inflection vs derivation, constituency, agreement, alignment, typology. Parse the sentence before arguing about it; the ambiguity is usually the bug. Seed: `curriculum/grammar.md`.
2. **LG1 - Formal logic.** Syllogistic, propositional and predicate logic, proof methods, soundness and completeness, the Godel limits. Validity is checkable; rhetoric is not. Seed: `curriculum/logic.md`.
3. **LG2 - Informal logic.** Fallacies, argument reconstruction, hidden premises, scope errors. One countermodel beats ten paragraphs of confidence. Seed: `curriculum/logic.md`.
4. **RH1 - Rhetoric.** Ethos, pathos, logos; the canons; stasis; the Toulmin layout. True claims still need a transport layer. Seed: `curriculum/rhetoric.md`.
5. **NU1 - Arithmetic.** Primes, unique factorization, modular arithmetic, induction, cardinality. Base rates and units kill more systems than data structures. Seed: `curriculum/arithmetic.md`.
6. **GE1 - Synthetic geometry.** Euclid's postulates, congruence, similarity, circle theorems, constructions, the independence of the parallel postulate. The diagram suggests; the postulates prove. Seed: `curriculum/geometry.md`.
7. **GE2 - Analytic geometry.** Coordinates, conics, vectors, transformations, invariants. Choose the system that makes the proof short. Seed: `curriculum/geometry.md`.
8. **MU1 - Music.** Ratios, tuning and the commas, scales and modes, harmony and voice leading, meter. Harmony is arithmetic you can hear. Seed: `curriculum/music.md`.
9. **AS1 - The celestial sphere.** Coordinates, time, seasons from tilt, phases, precession, magnitudes. The sky is the first clock; frame errors are the common bug. Seed: `curriculum/astronomy.md`.
10. **AS2 - Orbital mechanics.** Kepler's three laws, Newtonian gravitation, escape velocity, orbital elements and transfers. T in years and a in AU, or SI throughout; never mixed. Seed: `curriculum/astronomy.md`.

### Coin logic

1. **FN1 - Valuation.** Time value, NPV and IRR, DCF and terminal values, bonds, duration and convexity. Accept on NPV above zero; distrust terminal values even while pricing them. Seed: `curriculum/finance.md`.
2. **FN2 - Markets and instruments.** Equities, bonds, beta, Sharpe, splits and units. Price and yield move inversely; a split is the same cake cut thinner. Seed: `curriculum/finance.md`.
3. **FN3 - Derivatives.** Forwards, options, put-call parity, Black-Scholes assumptions, the Greeks. The model is a set of assumptions with prices attached. Seed: `curriculum/finance.md`.
4. **FN4 - Portfolio and risk.** Covariance not count, CAPM, factors, VaR and its tail, Kelly and fractional Kelly, drawdown arithmetic. Sizing by ruin beats sizing by greed. Seed: `curriculum/finance.md`.
5. **FN5 - Market microstructure.** Order books, spreads, adverse selection, impact, slippage, latency as rent. Speed is rented, the cage is owned. Seed: `curriculum/finance.md`.
6. **EC1 - Microeconomics.** Opportunity cost, elasticity, incidence, market structure, externalities, games. Find the incentive before naming the intent. Seed: `curriculum/economics.md`.
7. **EC2 - Macroeconomics.** GDP and the deflator, AS/AD, unemployment, the Phillips curve's instability, cycle dating. Policy has lags and expectations move. Seed: `curriculum/economics.md`.
8. **EC3 - Money and banking.** Central banks, policy rates, QE, MV = PY, Fisher, loans create deposits. Inflation is monetary over horizons, not over weekends. Seed: `curriculum/economics.md`.
9. **EC4 - Trade and international.** Comparative advantage, tariffs and deadweight loss, PPP as long-run anchor, the current account identity, parity conditions. Trade is not a contest of absolute productivity. Seed: `curriculum/economics.md`.
10. **EC5 - History of economic thought.** Smith, Ricardo, Keynes, Friedman, Kahneman and Tversky; each school a model with a domain. No school is the whole map; the map keeps the failures. Seed: `curriculum/economics.md`.

## The exam

Bank: `mcq_bank.json`. Runner: `exam.py`. Seeds: `curriculum/`. Stdlib only, no network, no deps.

Rules of the fire circle:

- 40 papers, 5 questions each, 200 questions total.
- 1 correct answer = 1 XP. Max XP = 200 = GOD LEVEL 200 OP.
- A paper passes at 4 of 5; a paper at 5 of 5 is OP. A paper without the pass is held as "in progress", never claimed.
- 200 of 200 is god level OP: every answer, every paper, nothing held back. 160+ is Cave Elder. 120+ is Rock Sage. 80+ is Stone Smith. 40+ is Pebble Apprentice. Below that, larva.

## Verification protocol (real world workings)

This is a character resume, and every line on it must still cash out in meatspace. The rule:

1. **Caveman rank is flavor.** It never counts as evidence.
2. **Real-world credential is the claim.** A degree, a certification, a prize with a record behind it.
3. **Evidence or it did not happen.** The proof column names the artifact. No artifact, no claim; the paper demotes to "training paper" and stays on the exam list only.
4. **Exams keep it honest.** The question bank tests the actual knowledge the credential stands for. Grak answers with the same rule as code: evidence pasted, or it did not happen.
5. **No hymn credentials.** A number that flatters a story is a hymn. A number checked against the record is a measurement. Every paper on this list is a measurement with an issuer and a proof artifact.
